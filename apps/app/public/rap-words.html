What is cached?

The aligned vocals, instrumental and word timestamps are reused. Finished MP3s are keyed by stem hashes, transcript, word list, gain and processing version. This is a single-track server experiment with a bounded disk cache, not a production CDN deployment.

Automatic transcript timestamps need listening review. Some vocals may remain in the instrumental; overlapping voices are muted together. Edits include 40 ms before / 70 ms after each word and 10 ms fades inside those padded boundaries. Both comparison mixes use the same fixed headroom gain.
